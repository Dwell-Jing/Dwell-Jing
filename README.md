## Selected Research

### [What Helps Denoising Can Hurt Memory: Rethinking History in Long Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00937)

**First author** · [Code](https://github.com/Dwell-Jing/commit-forcing)

We study how autoregressive video models use historical context during denoising and KV-cache writing. Commit Forcing is a training-free inference method that improves long-video consistency by changing how history is read when writing the cache.

### [VPEBench: The Image Plane Is an Instruction Surface for Image Editing](https://github.com/Dwell-Jing/vpebench)

**First author** · [Benchmark and code](https://github.com/Dwell-Jing/vpebench)

VPEBench evaluates image editing with visual instructions such as boxes, arrows, and curves. It covers 17 task types and 742 examples, with evaluation tools and judge prompts released alongside the benchmark.

### [Folding Language Models into Themselves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18113)

**Co-first author** · [Code](https://github.com/myboker/concertina)

Concertina compresses language models by folding adjacent layers into shared blocks and retaining layer-specific behavior with lightweight corrections.

### [Post-Training Leaves Behavioral Shadows on Unrelated Decisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29233)

**Second author** · [Code](https://github.com/myboker/ATD)

We investigate how post-training can affect seemingly unrelated decisions and study task-free distillation through a teacher's responses to carefully selected prompts.
